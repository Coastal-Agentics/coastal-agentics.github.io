Privacy

Effective October 3, 2026

The short version

This site does not use cookies, analytics, trackers, ads, forms or accounts. It runs no scripts and loads nothing from other companies' servers. We do not sell or share your information, because we do not collect it.

Who runs this site

Coastal Agentics is the planned name of a company in Savannah, Georgia, that has not been formed yet. Until it is, this site is run by Nye Warburton ("we", "us"). Questions about this page go to the contact route below.

What this site collects

Nothing, directly. The pages are plain HTML, CSS and images. There is no sign-up, no newsletter, no contact form, and nothing is saved in your browser.

Hosting: GitHub Pages

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit any GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes. We do not receive those logs and cannot see who visits. GitHub's handling of that data is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Links to other sites

Pages here link to GitHub and to our simulations at starscream-agentics.github.io. Those pages have their own privacy terms. The simulations site has its own privacy page.

Contacting us

For now, the way to reach us is a GitHub issue, which is public and covered by GitHub's own terms and privacy statement. If you write to us, we receive whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your message with anyone else, except the services that carry our messages, or when the law requires it. Ask us to delete your messages at any time.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

Because we do not collect personal information through this site, there is nothing to access, correct or delete here. For anything you have sent us, use the contact route below and we will help.

Changes

If what this site collects changes, for example if we add a contact form or analytics, we will update this page and its effective date before the change goes live.

Governing law

This page is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, USA.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't put personal information in them. We will publish a private email address here once our own domain is set up.
